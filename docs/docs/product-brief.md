## Project Goal
Create a professional website for Mogol Dierekliniek & Wilddienste to communicate their services, location, and expertise in both domestic and wildlife veterinary care.

## Core Services
1. **Domestic Animal Care**: Full-service animal clinic in Lephalale for the local community.
2. **Wildlife Services (Wilddienste)**:
   - **24/7 Support**: Emergency and scheduled wildlife assistance.
   - **Helicopter Tracking**: Specialized aerial wildlife management and tracking.
   - **Chemical Immobilization**: Safe and professional wildlife capture.
   - **Relocation/Restocking**: Expertise in "hervesting van wild".

## Design Preferences
- **Themes**: Bushveld, Savannah, Greenlands.
- **Aesthetics**: Premium, professional, and elite ("high-end nature").
- **Visual Identity**: 
    - Keep the **Black and White theme** for the branding/UI structure.
    - **Warm Environment**: Use "Bone," "Charcoal," and "Earth" tones within the B&W spectrum to ensure the clinic feels welcoming and warm, never sterile.
    - **Color Photography**: Use high-quality color images that showcase the *vibrancy* of life.
- **Tone & Voice**: 
    - **Individual Respect**: Refer to animals as **him/her** (never "it").
    - **Empathetic Authority**: Authoritative and expert, but grounded in deep empathy and care for every individual patient.

## Target Audience
- Pet owners in the Onwerwacht/Lephalale area.
- Game farmers and wildlife managers needing immobilization or relocation services.
