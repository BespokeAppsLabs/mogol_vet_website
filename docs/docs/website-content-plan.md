# Website Content Architecture: Mogol Dierekliniek & Wilddienste

This document defines the comprehensive content strategy, information architecture, and narrative flow for the Mogol website. It bridges the "Tactical Wildlife" elite services with the "Empathetic Clinic" care.

---

## 1. Information Architecture (Sitemap)

1.  **Home (Cinematic Narrative)**: The entry point to the brand's identity.
2.  **Emergency Ops (Wildlife)**: High-stakes 24/7 mission requests.
3.  **Livestock Excellence (Cattle & Equine)**: Professional management for farm productivity.
4.  **Clinic & Routine Care (Domestic)**: Empathetic, precision care for companion animals.
5.  **Services Hub**:
    *   Helicopter Tracking & Mobility.
    *   Livestock Herd Health & Diagnostics.
    *   Specialized Immobilization.
    *   Surgical Excellence.
6.  **Our Mission (About)**: Heritage, the "Tactical Timeline," and the Guardians narrative.

### 2.2. Emergency Operations (Wildlife)
**Goal:** High-speed mission intake for critical game farm situations.
**Tone:** Urgent, Precise, Operational.

*   **Header:** "TACTICAL READINESS: ACTIVE // ALL SYSTEMS OPERATIONAL".
*   **Step 1 (Location):** GPS Coordinate Capture. "Identify Sector or Farm Name."
*   **Step 2 (The Target):** Species Selection (Rhino, Leopard, Sable, etc.) and Condition (Snare, Predator, Unknown Trauma).
*   **Step 3 (Extraction/Response):** Urgency Level (Immediate Heli-Ops, 24hr Window, Scheduled Management).
*   **Confirmation:** "Mission Log Initialized. Dr. Kriel notified. ETA: [Variable] mins."

### 2.5. Universal Appointment Booking Flow
**Goal:** A seamless, empathetic intake process for both clinic and farm visits.
**Mechanism:** Multi-step Tactical Form.

**Step-by-Step Intake:**
1.  **Identify the Patient:**
    *   "Who are we treating?" (Name of him/her).
    *   Species (Dog, Cat, Horse, Cattle, Other).
2.  **Mission Objective:**
    *   Reason for visit (Routine Check-up, Medical Issue, Surgical Consultation, High-Tech Herd Work).
    *   Brief description of symptoms or goals.
3.  **Extraction Scaling (Timing):**
    *   "When is the mission needed?" (Date & Time selector).
4.  **Intel Capture:**
    *   Owner/Manager name and contact information.
5.  **Confirmation:**
    *   "Mission Scheduled. We are preparing for him/her. Await tactical confirmation via SMS."

## 3. Visual & Tonal Standards

### The "Him/Her" Directive
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Zero Tolerance Rule:** Never refer to a patient (animal) as "it". Every animal is an individual. Every automated email, booking confirmation, and web copy must use **him** or **her**.

### Photography Guidance
*   **Wildlife:** Fine art B&W. Use low-angle "hero shots" to give the animals a majestic, powerful presence. No "distressed" animal imagery—focus on the vibrancy of life and the precision of the care.
*   **Operational:** Tactical equipment, helicopter dash, ground crew collaboration. High-contrast, sharp focus.
*   **Clinic:** Warm "Bone" lighting. Close-up shots of hands (humanity) and medical precision (technology).

### Action Words
Use mission-oriented vocabulary:
*   *Deploy, Mission, Readiness, Tactical, Resilience, Guardian, Extraction, Parameters, Insight.*
